Lab 00.1 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

no VT-x/AMD-V flag→enable in BIOS, or use your own PC (VPS)
no IP after 5 min→virsh net-start default
snapshot fails with UEFI→keep BIOS boot (script default)
