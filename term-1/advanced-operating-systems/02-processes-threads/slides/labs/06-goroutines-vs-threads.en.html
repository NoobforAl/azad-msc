Lab 02.6 · Goal

Goroutines vs OS threads

Show that the kernel sees only OS threads and that Go multiplexes goroutines onto few of them, except during blocking syscalls.

Lab 02.6 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

syscall mode: few threads→os.File.Read uses the netpoller
pthread_create: EAGAIN→ulimit -u or cgroup pids.max
go: command not found→sudo apt install golang-go
