Lab 02.4 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

perf: No permission→perf_event_paranoid: use sudo
stress-ng: caught SIGILL→VM vCPU lacks an instruction: --cpu-method int64
bpftrace BTF error→needs /sys/kernel/btf/vmlinux
