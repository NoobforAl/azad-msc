Lab 03.3 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    Reader preference: writer waits the whole run
    a new reader is admitted while a writer waits
  • 2
    Writer preference: thousands of writes
    readers queue behind a waiting writer
  • 3
    Starvation is not deadlock
    readers progress; only the writer does not

Lab 03.3 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

PREFER_WRITER_NP changes nothing→use PREFER_WRITER_NONRECURSIVE_NP
no starvation visible→readers must overlap: ≥2 readers, no gaps
writer-pref deadlock→recursive read lock while a writer waits
