Lab 03.5 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    0/0 appears only in relaxed
    the store waited in the buffer while the load ran
  • 2
    The fence is a locked no-op
    lock orq $0x0,(%rsp)
  • 3
    Rust shows the same
    same hardware, same memory model (C++20)

Lab 03.5 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

relaxed shows 0→both threads on one CPU: check nproc / pinning
looking for mfence→gcc emits lock or: also a full barrier
counts vary→timing-dependent: compare orders of magnitude
