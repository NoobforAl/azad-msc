Lab 03.4 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    1 thread: zero futex calls
    every lock/unlock stayed in user space
  • 2
    WAIT errors = EAGAIN
    value changed before the kernel checked it
  • 3
    op 0 = WAIT, op 1 = WAKE
    op & 127 strips FUTEX_PRIVATE_FLAG

Lab 03.4 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

hangs under contention→unlock must wake when old value was 2
strace changes the result→tracing slows threads; compare with the program's own counters
more WAKE than WAIT→state 2 is sticky: expected
