Deadlock: create, read, detect

Create an AB-BA deadlock, read it from gdb and /proc, detect the inverted order with TSan and helgrind, fix it with address ordering.

Steps

Lab 03.6 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    Both threads sleep in futex_wait
    state S, 0% CPU: a deadlock does not spin
  • 2
    TSan reports without a hang
    lock-order-inversion (potential deadlock)
  • 3
    Lockdep: same idea in the kernel
    report UNVERIFIED: VM kernel lacks PROVE_LOCKING

Common pitfalls

lockdep silent→grep PROVE_LOCKING /boot/config-$(uname -r)
