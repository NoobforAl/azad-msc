Lab 03.2 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

if instead of while→breaks with 2 consumers or spurious wake-ups
hang at shutdown→poison pill per consumer, or closed flag + broadcast
Go: all goroutines are asleep→producer must close(ch)
