Lab 06.5 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    cpuid is many syscalls
    the price is the exit, not the instruction
  • 2
    Guest cannot count exits
    only the host sees kvm:kvm_exit
  • 3
    Two hypervisors, two numbers
    compare with WSL2 (Hyper-V): ~384 ns

Lab 06.5 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

hypervisor present = 0→bare metal: cpuid does not exit
noisy cpuid numbers→run 3x, take the min; taskset -c 2
perf stat -e cycles: <not supported>→no PMU in this guest: use task-clock
