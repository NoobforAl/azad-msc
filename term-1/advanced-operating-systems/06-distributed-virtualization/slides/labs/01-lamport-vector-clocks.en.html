Lab 06.1 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    lamport n/n, vector n/n
    merge() makes every receive larger than its send
  • 2
    Ties in L across processes
    total order breaks ties by process id
  • 3
    L(a) < L(b) yet a ∥ b
    sorting logs by Lamport time is not causal order
