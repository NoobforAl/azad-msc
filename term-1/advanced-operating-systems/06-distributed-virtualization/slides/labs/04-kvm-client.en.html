Lab 06.4 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

open /dev/kvm: No such file→bare metal or nested VM; modprobe kvm_intel
KVM_EXIT_FAIL_ENTRY 0x80000021→invalid guest state: KVM_GET_SREGS first
KVM_EXIT_SHUTDOWN→RIP outside the memory slot
