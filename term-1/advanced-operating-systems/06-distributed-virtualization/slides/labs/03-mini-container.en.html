Lab 06.3 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

pivot_root: Invalid argument→mount --bind rootfs rootfs; mount --make-rprivate /
ps shows host processes→mount /proc after pivot_root
hog swaps instead of dying→echo 0 > memory.swap.max
