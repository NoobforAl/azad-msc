Lab 05.6 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

Invalid argument from dmsetup→sudo modprobe dm-flakey
device busy on remove→umount first; dmsetup remove; losetup -d
everything survived→dmsetup suspend without --nolockfs = fs freeze = sync
