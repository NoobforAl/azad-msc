Lab 05.2 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    Warm is an order of magnitude faster
    memcpy vs disk + readahead
  • 2
    Folios added only on the cold read
    mm_filemap_add_to_page_cache count
  • 3
    Async readahead dominates
    page_cache_async_ra >> sync_ra

Lab 05.2 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

drop_caches on a shared VM→evict one file with fadvise
evict does nothing→dirty or mmapped pages: fdatasync first
cold is suspiciously fast→host-side disk cache in the hypervisor
