Lab 05.3 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    fsync per record costs ~10 000x
    journal commit + cache flush each time
  • 2
    Dirty waits 30 s, then drains
    below background ratio: dirty_expire_centisecs
  • 3
    Small BDI budget = many short pauses
    tracepoint writeback:balance_dirty_pages

Lab 05.3 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

changing vm.dirty_* on a shared VM→use per-BDI knobs on your loop device
fsync looks free→tmpfs or nobarrier: check findmnt -T .
loop device left behind→losetup -a; losetup -d /dev/loopN
