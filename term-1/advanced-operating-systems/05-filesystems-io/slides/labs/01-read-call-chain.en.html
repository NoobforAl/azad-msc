Lab 05.1 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

VM freezes: soft lockup [trace-cmd]→always -l/-g, depth <= 6, timeout 10, reset
kprobe not found→bpftrace -l 'kprobe:filemap_read*'
cold read shows 0 bios→sync the file first; dirty pages stay
