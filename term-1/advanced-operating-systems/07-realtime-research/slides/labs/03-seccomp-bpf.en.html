Lab 07.3 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

seccomp_load: EACCES→prctl(PR_SET_NO_NEW_PRIVS) first
open rule never fires→libc calls openat — strace first
allow-list → segfault→forgot futex: glibc can't handle ENOSYS there
