Lab 07.2 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    H waits 977.6 ms → 181.6 ms
    the difference is M's 800 ms
  • 2
    Kernel boost 89 → 69
    prio = 99 - rt_priority · rt_mutex_setprio()
  • 3
    Only PI uses FUTEX_LOCK_PI
