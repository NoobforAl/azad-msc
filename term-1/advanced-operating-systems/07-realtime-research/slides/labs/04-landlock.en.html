Lab 07.4 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    Denied after path lookup
    openat ... = -1 EACCES (LSM hook, not seccomp)
  • 2
    /dev/null is a file too
    WRITE_FILE is handled → granted nowhere but /tmp/ll-rw

Lab 07.4 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

restrict_self: Operation not permitted→prctl(PR_SET_NO_NEW_PRIVS) first
create_ruleset: Invalid argument→drop rights the running ABI doesn't know
writes still work somewhere→unhandled rights stay allowed
