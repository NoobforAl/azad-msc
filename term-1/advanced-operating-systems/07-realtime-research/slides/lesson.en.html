SAY (EN): cyclictest measures from the planned wake-up time to step 5. Steps 1, 2 and 4 cost microseconds. 07 · Priority inversion

Inheritance keeps M from overtaking L

SAY (EN): ① Without a protocol, M preempts the lock holder L, so H waits for M too. ② With inheritance, L runs at H's priority until it unlocks; H waits only for the rest of L's critical section, and M runs after. SAY (FA): ① بدون پروتکل، M صاحب قفل یعنی L را کنار می‌زند، پس H منتظر M هم می‌ماند. ② با inheritance، ‏L تا unlock با اولویت H اجرا می‌شود؛ ‏H فقط منتظر باقی بخش بحرانی L است و M بعد از آن اجرا می‌شود. KEY POINT: PI bounds the inversion; it does not remove waiting for L. EXAMPLE: Mars Pathfinder 1997: VxWorks select() semaphore without PI → repeated resets. PITFALL: Default pthread mutexes have no PI. ASK: Why do we need three priorities to see the problem? TIME: 120

07 · PREEMPT_RT

PREEMPT_RT changes the locks, not just the preemption points

PREEMPT_DYNAMIC (full)

  • spin_lock(): spins, preemption off
  • IRQ handlers in hard-IRQ context
  • softirqs with bottom halves off
  • switch at runtime: none / voluntary / full / lazy

PREEMPT_RT

  • spin_lock() = rt_mutex: sleeps, PI
  • IRQ threads irq/N, FIFO 50
  • softirqs in threads, local locks
  • compile time only · uname -v shows it

Only raw_spinlock_t still spins: scheduler, IRQ and timer core.

SAY (EN): kernel/locking/spinlock_rt.c implements spin_lock as an rt_mutex with migrate_disable(); include/linux/interrupt.h hard-wires force_irqthreads() to true under PREEMPT_RT. Our VM is PREEMPT_DYNAMIC, so RT numbers in this module are UNVERIFIED; build the --rt flavor from 00-setup lab 03. SAY (FA): ‏kernel/locking/spinlock_rt.c تابع spin_lock را به شکل یک rt_mutex همراه با migrate_disable() پیاده می‌کند؛ ‏include/linux/interrupt.h زیر PREEMPT_RT مقدار force_irqthreads() را ثابتِ true می‌کند. VM ما PREEMPT_DYNAMIC است، پس عددهای RT در این ماژول UNVERIFIED‌اند؛ گونهٔ ‎--rt‎ را با آزمایش ۰۳ از 00-setup بسازید. KEY POINT: Kconfig: depends on EXPERT && ARCH_SUPPORTS_RT && !COMPILE_TEST. EXAMPLE: init/Makefile L30: preempt-flag-$(CONFIG_PREEMPT_RT) := PREEMPT_RT PITFALL: There is no /sys/kernel/realtime in v6.18.63. TIME: 120

07 · Linux vs OpenBSD

Flexible filters vs one-line promises

seccomp + Landlock

  • BPF over syscall nr + raw args
  • filter author maps syscalls
  • errno / trap / notify / kill
  • always inherited across exec

pledge + unveil

  • pledge("stdio rpath") promise bits
  • kernel table pledge_syscalls[]
  • uncatchable SIGABRT + core
  • separate execpromises

pledge_syscall() is one bitwise AND: p_pledge & pledge_syscalls[code].

SAY (EN): openbsd/sys/kern/kern_pledge.c pledge_syscall() at 8cada8c, L537-554, called from mi_syscall in sys/sys/syscall_mi.h L167-172. OpenBSD keeps the syscall-to-category map in the kernel; Linux leaves it to the filter author. unveil_check_final() in kern_unveil.c is the Landlock counterpart. SAY (FA): ‏openbsd/sys/kern/kern_pledge.c تابع pledge_syscall() در 8cada8c خط ۵۳۷ تا ۵۵۴ که از mi_syscall در sys/sys/syscall_mi.h خط ۱۶۷ تا ۱۷۲ صدا زده می‌شود. ‏OpenBSD نگاشت syscall به دسته را در کرنل نگه می‌دارد؛ لینوکس آن را به نویسندهٔ فیلتر می‌سپارد. ‏unveil_check_final() در kern_unveil.c همتای Landlock است. KEY POINT: Same monotonic principle: restrictions only tighten. EXAMPLE: A new Linux syscall is silently allowed by a deny-list filter. PITFALL: OpenBSD has no SCHED_FIFO at all (git grep in sys/ finds none). TIME: 90

Quick check

A SCHED_FIFO 30 thread waits on a default pthread mutex held by a FIFO 10 thread. What bounds its wait?

A The FIFO 10 thread's critical section
B Nothing: medium tasks can run first
C The futex timeout
D SCHED_DEADLINE admission
SAY (EN): Twenty seconds. Look at the word "default". SAY (FA): بیست ثانیه. به کلمهٔ «پیش‌فرض» دقت کنید. KEY POINT: Answer on the next slide. EXAMPLE: — PITFALL: — ASK: Who picked A? What would make A true? TIME: 45
Answer: B

Without a protocol, inversion is unbounded

A default mutex is PTHREAD_PRIO_NONE: any FIFO 11–29 task can preempt the owner while the FIFO 30 waiter blocks.

A becomes true with PTHREAD_PRIO_INHERIT → futex_lock_pi() → rt_mutex (lab 02)

SAY (EN): With PTHREAD_PRIO_INHERIT the owner runs at 30 and the wait is bounded by its critical section: that is answer A. C is wrong: pthread_mutex_lock has no timeout. D is unrelated. SAY (FA): با PTHREAD_PRIO_INHERIT صاحب قفل با اولویت ۳۰ اجرا می‌شود و انتظار به بخش بحرانی آن محدود است: این گزینهٔ A است. ‏C غلط است: pthread_mutex_lock مهلت زمانی ندارد. ‏D بی‌ربط است. KEY POINT: Bounded inversion is normal; unbounded is a bug. EXAMPLE: inversion.c none vs inherit. PITFALL: — TIME: 60
