Lab 04.6 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

mkdir: Permission denied→needs sudo (script does it)
pass 1: 0 major faults→pages charged elsewhere: fadvise DONTNEED
hog reaches 512 MiB→shell not in the cgroup
