Lab 04.2 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

pfn=0x0→not root (hidden since 4.2)
Shared_Dirty = 0 after fork→THP pages; keep MADV_NOHUGEPAGE
faults ≠ 8192→a few stack/printf pages; small diff is fine
