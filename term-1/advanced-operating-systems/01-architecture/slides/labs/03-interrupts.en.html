Lab 01.3 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    Named vectors ≠ device IRQs
    LOC/CAL/RES bypass irq_handler_entry (3,650)
  • 2
    LOC ≈ 220/s per CPU, HZ=1000
    NO_HZ: idle CPUs skip ticks
  • 3
    134,260 page faults, none here
    exceptions are not interrupts
