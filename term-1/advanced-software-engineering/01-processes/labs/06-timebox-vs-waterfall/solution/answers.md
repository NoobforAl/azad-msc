# Lab 01.6 — reference answers

The reports `expected-overrun-0.txt`, `expected-overrun-30.txt` and `expected-overrun-60.txt` are the output of `python plan_compare.py --overrun 0 | 0.3 | 0.6`.

1. **Overrun at which the waterfall misses 9 weeks.** The waterfall length is `100 x (1 + overrun) / 12` weeks (100 developer-days of estimate, 12 developer-days per week), which equals 9 weeks at `overrun = 108/100 - 1 = 0.08`. At 0.08 it ends at week 9.0 (meets), at 0.09 at week 9.1 (misses by 0.1 weeks). The phases do not matter for the end date, only the total effort does.
2. **Why the timebox delivers fewer items there.** The three sprints hold 3 x 36 = 108 developer-days, exactly the waterfall's 9 weeks of capacity, and at 8% overrun the real effort is exactly 108. But the timebox cannot use every day: sprint 1 commits four items worth 36 days by estimate, which really need 38.9, so REG-04 is cut with about 2.9 days still to do; in sprint 2 only 30.7 of the 36 days are committed, because the next item (6 days) does not fit into the 5.3 days that remain after the finished and the planned work, so those days stay unused. The last sprint then runs out of time with REG-12 unfinished (an item that is only partly done at the deadline counts as not delivered). In a waterfall the 108 days are one pool, so everything finishes when the pool is empty.
3. **Sentences for the client (30% overrun).** Timeboxed: by week 3 you can use the login, enrollment and drop-course features (50% of the value points); by week 6 also the waitlist, prerequisite checks and the admin screens (76%); by week 9 you have 10 of 12 items (96%), without the mobile layout and the dean's reports, which were the lowest priorities. Waterfall: you have nothing usable before week 10.8; at the week-9 deadline you have no release at all.

Caveats worth writing in an answer: all numbers come from invented estimates and invented value points; the model has no requirements changes, no integration problems and no sprint overhead; the waterfall could also be re-planned to drop scope, but then the decision comes late and is not made by the process itself.
