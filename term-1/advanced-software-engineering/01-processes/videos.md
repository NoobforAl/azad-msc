## Gaps (no verified video found)

I found no verifiable video for these topics, so none is listed. Use the written sources instead.

- **Brooks's "No Silver Bullet" by Brooks himself.** Use the 1986 paper and Schach 8e ch. 1. Row 6 is the nearest video.
- **Parnas, "A Rational Design Process: How and Why to Fake It."** The only Parnas video that turned up is a Vimeo lecture on precise documentation, which is off-topic. Read the paper (IEEE TSE, 1986).
- **Waterfall, V-model and spiral model lectures.** A search for Boehm's spiral model found only an ACM Tech Talk page and a Microsoft webcast notice, neither of which I opened as a playable video. Use Schach 8e ch. 2 §2.9.
- **MIT 6.031, CMU 17-313 / 17-214, Stanford CS190.** No recording on the process topics turned up in my searches. CMU 17-313 publishes slides at `cmu-313.github.io`, but I found no lecture videos there.
- **Scrum and Kanban from their authors.** A Kniberg talk exists on Vimeo ("Kanban and Scrum - making the most of both") but I didn't open it. Use his free minibook instead.
