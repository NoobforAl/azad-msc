Lab 01.3 · What to observe

What to observe

  • 1
    Coding is a small slice
    -10% coding = 0.85-1.19% of total; -10% maintenance = 6.7-7.5%
  • 2
    Inspections move cost left
    faults found by users: 19% → 11%
  • 3
    Small runs are noisy
    1000 faults: ~5% off the expected value; 100,000: 0.1%
  • 4
    It all hangs on the cost curve
    with r = 1 inspections save nothing

Lab 01.3 · Common pitfalls

Common pitfalls

treating the output as data→multipliers and rates are assumptions
quoting 57% as Schach's number→model counts fault fixing only; book: 67-75% of life-cycle cost
--multipliers rejected→exactly six comma-separated numbers
