01 · Processes · economics

A 10% saving in maintenance is worth about nine times a 10% saving in coding

Schach 8e, ch. 1 §1.3.2, p. 12: coding with unit testing is 34% of the 25% development share (8.5% of total); maintenance is 75%.

SAY (EN): Schach's data say most of the lifetime cost is postdelivery maintenance. So a technique that makes coding 10% cheaper saves at most 0.85% of everything, while one that makes maintenance 10% cheaper saves about 7.5%. 7.5 over 0.85 is about nine. SAY (FA): داده‌های Schach می‌گویند بیشتر هزینهٔ عمر نرم‌افزار در نگهداری پس از تحویل است. پس تکنیکی که کدنویسی را ۱۰٪ ارزان‌تر کند حداکثر ۰٫۸۵٪ کل هزینه را صرفه‌جویی می‌کند، ولی تکنیکی که نگهداری را ۱۰٪ ارزان‌تر کند حدود ۷٫۵٪. نسبت ۷٫۵ به ۰٫۸۵ حدود نه است. KEY POINT: Where the money goes decides which process decisions matter. EXAMPLE: 0.10 × 8.5% = 0.85% and 0.10 × 75% = 7.5% (lab 03 reproduces both). PITFALL: Applying the 34%-of-25% share to your own team without measuring it; it is an industry average from older data. ASK: What would you measure in your own repository to find your coding share? 01 · Processes · vocabulary

Projects iterate and increment in every workflow

1Each build adds a piece
2Each artifact gets better
3Every build runs the tests
SAY (EN): Schach's iterative-and-incremental model is the baseline he compares everything with. Columns are builds, rows are workflows. Implementation adds a feature each build, which is incrementation. Requirements, model and design get a new version, which is iteration. Each build includes the test workflow, so faults are found while they are cheap. SAY (FA): مدل iterative-and-incremental از Schach مبنایی است که همه‌چیز را با آن مقایسه می‌کند. ستون‌ها buildها هستند و ردیف‌ها workflowها. پیاده‌سازی در هر build یک ویژگی اضافه می‌کند که incrementation است. نیازمندی‌ها، مدل و طراحی نسخهٔ تازه می‌گیرند که iteration است. هر build شامل workflow تست است، پس خطاها زمانی پیدا می‌شوند که ارزان‌اند. KEY POINT: Different questions: "how can this be better?" (iteration) versus "what do we add next?" (incrementation). EXAMPLE: Schach 8e, ch. 2 §2.5 and §2.7. The labels (reqs v2, + feature B) are illustrative, not from the book. PITFALL: Treating the two words as synonyms. ASK: Your last three releases: did they only add, or did they also rework the design? TIME: 90
01 · Processes · in a Git repository

Iteration improves an artifact; incrementation adds a piece

Iteration

  • Rewrite the repository layer after a load test
  • A revised design document
  • Refactoring commits
  • Asks: how can this be better?

Incrementation

  • Tagged releases v0.2.0, v0.3.0
  • Each build adds functionality
  • A feature merged weekly with passing tests
  • Asks: what do we add next?
SAY (EN): In a repository, increments are the tagged releases that each add an endpoint. Iterations are the commits that make the same artifact better. A project that only increments piles up design debt; one that only iterates never ships. SAY (FA): در یک مخزن، incrementها همان releaseهای tag‌شده‌اند که هر کدام یک endpoint اضافه می‌کنند. iterationها commitهایی‌اند که همان artifact را بهتر می‌کنند. پروژه‌ای که فقط increment کند بدهی طراحی جمع می‌کند و پروژه‌ای که فقط iterate کند هرگز تحویل نمی‌دهد. KEY POINT: Real work does both, workflow by workflow. EXAMPLE: "Add a new endpoint every sprint" is incrementation; it becomes iteration only when the design is reworked. PITFALL: Calling every release an iteration. TIME: 60
01 · Processes · models

Plan-driven control versus agile feedback

Plan-driven: waterfall, V-model

  • Control: documents and sign-off per phase
  • Fits: stable requirements, fixed-scope contract
  • Fails: the wrong product is found late
  • In a repo: required reviews and status checks

Agile: XP, Scrum, Kanban

  • Control: a short feedback loop
  • Fits: unclear needs, client reachable daily
  • Fails: evidence is mostly observational
  • In a repo: CI on every push, WIP limits
SAY (EN): Plan-driven models answer "how do we keep a 200-person project under control" with order and documents. Agile answers "what if we do not know what the client needs" with a short feedback loop. Neither is the right answer everywhere, and most real projects mix them. SAY (FA): مدل‌های plan-driven به پرسش «یک پروژهٔ دویست‌نفره را چطور کنترل کنیم» با نظم و مستندات پاسخ می‌دهند. agile به پرسش «اگر نمی‌دانیم مشتری چه می‌خواهد چه کنیم» با حلقهٔ بازخورد کوتاه. هیچ‌کدام در همه‌جا درست نیست و بیشتر پروژه‌های واقعی ترکیبی‌اند. KEY POINT: Choose from the situation, then mix: agile inside, plan-driven gates around. EXAMPLE: A hardware or regulatory gate in the middle of a project calls for a mix. PITFALL: "Agile means no plan and no documents." The Manifesto values working software over comprehensive documentation, not instead of it (Schach 8e, ch. 2 §2.9.5). Evidence for agile versus plan-driven is `OPINION / CONTESTED`. TIME: 75
01 · Processes · a classic source

The waterfall people criticise is the first picture in Royce's paper

Royce draws the straight-line sequence, then says that implementing that concept "is risky and invites failure".

Royce 1970, "Managing the Development of Large Software Systems", IEEE WESCON, pp. 1-9 · Schach 8e, ch. 2 §2.9.2

SAY (EN): Schach credits Royce with the waterfall. The paper itself shows the simple sequence first, calls it risky because testing at the end is the first time timing and storage are experienced, and then recommends five additions, including doing the job twice and involving the customer. SAY (FA): Schach مدل waterfall را به Royce نسبت می‌دهد. خود مقاله اول توالی ساده را نشان می‌دهد، آن را پرریسک می‌نامد چون تست در پایان اولین باری است که زمان‌بندی و حافظه تجربه می‌شود، و بعد پنج افزودنی پیشنهاد می‌کند، از جمله دوبار انجام دادن کار و درگیر کردن مشتری. KEY POINT: The model people criticise is the first figure of a paper that argues for iteration and a prototype. EXAMPLE: Royce's five additions: program design first, document the design, do it twice, plan and monitor testing, involve the customer. Opened as a reprint on 2026-11-23 (third-party copy). PITFALL: Citing Boehm's 1988 spiral paper as if it were read: it was NOT opened (UNVERIFIED beyond Schach's summary, §2.9.7). TIME: 75

The merge window lasts for approximately two weeks.  At the end of thistime, Linus Torvalds will declare that the window is closed and release thefirst of the "rc" kernels.  For the kernel which is destined to be 5.6,for example, the release which happens at the end of the merge window willbe called 5.6-rc1. OpenBSD says when a style rule applies

openbsd/share/man/man9/style.9 · DESCRIPTION8cada8c · L38-43
These guidelines should be followed for all new code.In general, code can be considered.Dq new codewhen it makes up about 50% or more of the file(s) involved.This is enough to break precedents in the existing code and use thecurrent style guidelines.

2New code: 50% or more of the file

SAY (EN): The standard is a manual page, style(9), in the same system as every kernel interface. It tells you when the rule applies: new code follows the current style, and "new code" means half or more of the files involved. That is an explicit policy for style debt. Linux's coding-style says the same thing as a preference with a rationale, and ships a .clang-format file. SAY (FA): استاندارد یک صفحهٔ manual به نام style(9) است، در همان سامانه‌ای که هر interface کرنل. می‌گوید قاعده کِی اعمال می‌شود: کد جدید از سبک فعلی پیروی می‌کند و «کد جدید» یعنی نیمی یا بیشتر از فایل‌های درگیر. این یک سیاست صریح برای بدهی سبک است. coding-style در Linux همین را به‌صورت ترجیح همراه با دلیل می‌گوید و فایل .clang-format هم دارد. KEY POINT: A coding standard is a process artifact; the carrier differs (a man page versus a document plus a tool config). EXAMPLE: In the man-page source, .Dq is the macro that prints double quotes around "new code". 01 · Processes · choosing

Choose a model from the situation, then mix

Stable, safety-critical

Plan-driven: V-model, waterfall with iteration

Large, technically risky

Spiral, or Unified Process elaboration

Unclear needs, client on call

Agile: XP or Scrum

Unpredictable work arrival

Kanban: flow and WIP limits

Hardware or regulatory gate in the middle: agile inside, plan-driven gates around.

SAY (EN): Schach's own advice is that each organization should choose the model that fits its management, employees and process, and that the iterative-and-incremental model is closest to reality. Use the table to start the conversation, then mix. SAY (FA): توصیهٔ خود Schach این است که هر سازمان مدلی را برگزیند که با مدیریت، کارکنان و فرایندش جور است، و اینکه مدل iterative-and-incremental به واقعیت نزدیک‌تر است. جدول را برای شروع گفتگو به کار ببرید و بعد ترکیب کنید. KEY POINT: A mix-and-match model is normal, not a failure of discipline. EXAMPLE: Schach 8e, ch. 2 §2.10. PITFALL: Scrum is a management framework for timeboxed iterations, not a life-cycle model: it says nothing about how you test or architect. TIME: 60
